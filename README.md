# GameCo Regional Sales Analysis (Excel)

## Overview

This project is an Excel-based market analysis of historical video game sales for **GameCo**, focused on understanding how sales behavior differs across major geographic markets and how those regional patterns changed over time.

The analysis was structured as an end-to-end business workflow: preparing the dataset in Excel, cleaning and validating the data, building pivot-table summaries, performing exploratory and descriptive analysis, developing insights, creating visualizations, and presenting findings in a stakeholder-facing format.

The final business question focused on whether GameCo’s major regions had maintained the same share of global sales over time, and whether the company should reconsider how it allocates marketing budget for **2017 and beyond** based on the 2016 planning cycle.

---

## Business Context

In the project scenario, GameCo’s executive board was planning the **2017 marketing budget** in **October 2016**. Their working assumption was that regional sales behavior had remained broadly stable over time. The purpose of this analysis was to test that assumption using historical sales data and determine whether changes in regional sales mix justified a revised marketing approach.

This project was built to support an executive audience, which meant the outputs needed to be clear, concise, and business-relevant rather than purely technical.

---

## Project Objectives

This project was designed to answer questions such as:

- How have the proportions of global sales generated by **North America, Europe, and Japan** changed over time?
- Are there meaningful regional differences in sales mix by genre?
- Which patterns in the data challenge GameCo’s current assumptions?
- What action should GameCo consider for future marketing allocation?

Along the way, the project also explored sales behavior by **genre, year, publisher, platform, and region** using pivot tables, grouping logic, calculated fields, descriptive statistics, and charts.
